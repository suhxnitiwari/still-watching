    About this data

    Netflix lets anyone download their viewing activity: one row per title with the date it was watched. Mine runs from to . A Python pipeline splits each title into show, season and episode, separates series from movies, and marks when I lived in Dallas and when I moved to Austin.

    What it can't see. The export keeps only the latest date for each title, so a rewatched episode counts once, on the last day I watched it. It has dates but no times, so it can't tell when in the day I watched. It only knows this profile: anything I watched on someone else's account isn't here.

    Privacy. The raw history never leaves my laptop. This site ships per-show and per-month summaries only.

    Images. Show posters and backgrounds come from TVmaze and belong to their studios and networks. The moving poster wall is built from the shows in my own history.
